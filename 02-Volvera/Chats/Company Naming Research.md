I checked several of the obvious candidates and killed three before writing this — **Kestrel**, **Alacrity**, and **Throughline** are all occupied by AI/consulting firms doing adjacent work. That's the real constraint: single-word English nouns are effectively gone in this category. Everything below is either an unusual compound or an obscure term, because that's what's still ownable.

**The four tests I scored against**

1. **Stretch** — you sell speed-to-lead *and* offer engineering *and* hiring pipelines. Don't name it after the wedge product.
2. **Phone test** — you're cold-calling SMBs. It has to be spellable and sayable in one pass.
3. **Ownability** — descriptive names ("Lead Velocity Systems") can't be trademarked and vanish in search.
4. **Doesn't date** — "___ AI" will read like "___ Dot Com" by 2029. Your moat is the loop, not the model.

Verdict on *Arrange AI*: weak. "Arrange" is a soft verb that implies scheduling only, and the AI suffix caps it.

**The honest root first** — because the spin is stronger when the etymology is real, and this one is unusually generous.

*Volvere* (Latin): to turn, to roll, to revolve. It's the root of **revolve, evolve, involve, revolution** — and of **volume**, from *volumen*, a rolled scroll. In Spanish and Portuguese it survives as *volver*: to turn, and to **return**. "Volverá" means *they will come back*.

You don't have to stretch anything. Pick which of these you lead with.

---

**Spin 1 — The turn (cleanest)**

Every business has leads sitting still. You make them turn.

> "Volvera means *to turn*. Turning a lead into a call, a call into a customer, a customer into revenue. That's the whole business."

This maps directly onto your four levers. It also gives you a verb: *"we turn your pipeline."*

**Spin 2 — The return (strongest commercially)**

In Spanish, *volverá* means **it will come back**. Your entire product is about the lead that doesn't get lost — and the money that comes back.

> "In Latin it means to turn. In Spanish it means *they come back*. That's what we sell — the leads you were losing, returning."

This one does double duty as an ROI line, and it plays well in Florida.

> "Same root as revolve and evolve — a system that goes around, and gets better every time it does."

**Spin 4 — Volume**

*Volumen* — a rolled scroll — is where the English word volume comes from. Volume is one of your four levers. Small detail, but it's a nice line on an About page.

**Spin 5 — The Brain**
