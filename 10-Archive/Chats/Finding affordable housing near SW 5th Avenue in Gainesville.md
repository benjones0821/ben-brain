One naming note first: the 4x4 cluster you're describing is on **SW 5th Avenue** (runs east–west, 1000–1300 blocks, behind Norman Hall next to Sorority Row). SW 5th *Street* is a north–south road over by Depot Park with essentially no student 4x4 inventory. Everything below is the 5th Ave corridor.

**The timing problem you need to know about**

It's September 9, 2026. Fall 2026 leases are done — Trimark's own site says "Sold Out for Fall 2026," and The Row is already running a Fall '27 VIP list. So you're in one of two lanes:

- **Move in now/spring** → sublease or "relet" market only. Trimark runs a public sublease board, and UF's off-campus site has a sublet filter.
- **Fall 2027** → pre-leasing is open right now, and this is the cheap window. These properties use tier pricing that climbs as beds fill, so a 4x4 under $1,200 is realistic in September and much harder by January.

**Directly on SW 5th Ave**

- **The Row on 5th** — 1130 SW 5th Ave. Your best bet. Private bathroom for every bedroom, 2–4 bed layouts, listings show it starting around **$1,125–$1,155/bed**. Furniture is +$60/bed/mo here (not included like their other buildings). Leasing office is at 407 SW 13th St, (352) 559-3400.
- **UFORA** — 1218 SW 5th Ave. Four separate 4x4 plans (D1–D4, 1,268–1,418 sq ft), every bedroom has its own bath. But rates ran roughly $1,200–$2,000+/person as of early 2026, so you'd be at the very top of budget. Also worth reading the Google reviews — 3.4 stars with real complaints about maintenance and management.
- **Savion Park** — 719 SW 5th Ave. Has a 4BR/4BA with an 18' porch. Quieter, near Innovation Square, but zero amenities (no gym or pool).
- **Woodbury Row** — buildings at 1011 SW 5th Ave and 1128 SW 6th Ave. Private bath per bedroom, rent includes internet + W/D + trash. Zumper shows a $1,100–$2,000 range. **No pets**, and 4.5 stars with unusually good reviews for Gainesville.

**One to two blocks off**

Sabal Palms (1013 SW 7th Ave), Solaria (1024 SW 7th Ave), The Row on 7th (1114 SW 7th Ave), Cascades (925 SW 4th Ave), Tuscana (811 SW 8th St) — all have true 4BR/4BA plans. The Row on 7th usually prices under The Row on 5th for the same product. Solaria and Tuscana also sell 4/3 layouts, so say "4x4" explicitly or you'll get quoted the wrong plan.

**Skip or downgrade**

Latitude 29 is 1–2BR only. The Courtyards (1231 SW 3rd Ave) is the cheap option at ~$710/bed but it's shared-bath, not a 4x4 — and 3.5 stars with recurring maintenance complaints.

**What I'd actually do**

Call two numbers today: **(352) 559-3400** for The Row on 5th/7th and **(352) 376-6223** for Trimark's whole portfolio. Ask each for Fall 2027 4x4 rates *and* any current relet in a 4x4. Trimark's tour form has a "$900–$1,100 per BR" bracket — pick it and make them work inside it.

Two caveats worth stating plainly: I can't see live unit-level inventory, and student-housing rates here change weekly with tier pricing, so treat every number above as a starting point to verify. Also ask what's *not* in the quoted rent — The Row charges a $200/bedroom deposit, and several of these bill electric and water separately through GRU and a third-party provider, which adds real money to a $1,200 target.
