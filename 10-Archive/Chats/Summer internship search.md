"GCS" almost certainly stands for **Google Customer Solutions**, Google's small and mid-sized business ads sales organization. GCS sales teams act as advisors and sellers who bring Google's products to small- and medium-sized businesses, working directly with company owners to help them grow.

**How US GCS interns get hired:** there usually isn't a standalone "GCS Intern" posting in the US. GCS is one of the teams business interns get placed on. Historically this was Google's BOLD program: salary sites track the role as "Google Customer Solutions - BOLD Intern", and BOLD interns have been placed on teams such as Sales, Services and Support, Marketing, People Operations, gTech, and Finance. You apply to the general business internship and indicate that you're interested in sales. One intern described the application this way: you list your skill areas, choose up to three preferred locations, and pick a preferred area such as Sales, with no essay question.

**Where to look:**
- Google's [Business internships page](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/students/business-internships/) is where US business and sales intern roles are posted. Google notes that the roles listed there are a sample rather than a complete list.
- The closest GCS-specific posting I found is the [Customer Growth Associate BS/MS Intern, 2026](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/73653158101820102-customer-growth-associate-bsms-intern-2026). Those interns join the Scaled GCS sales team and support small and medium-sized businesses across EMEA, so it's the European version, not the San Francisco role.

I couldn't find the exact US Summer 2026 listing, which has probably been taken down since the internship already ended. Summer 2027 postings are appearing now. For example, one Google Summer 2027 intern listing says applications must be completed before October 9, 2026 and are reviewed on a rolling basis, so it's worth checking the business internships page soon.

The Summer 2027 version is the **[Business Undergraduate Intern, Summer 2027](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/100460871561421510-business-undergraduate-intern-summer-2027)** posting, and it's open now. The deadline is **October 9**, about two weeks from today.

- **What it is:** a paid summer internship for undergraduates who want non-technical roles at Google. Interns are placed on Sales, Marketing, or Finance teams. GCS is part of the Sales track. The posting doesn't name GCS specifically, so you can't apply to it directly, but after the application window closes Google may contact you for more information and you'll get a chance to state which function you prefer.
- **Locations:** you can list preferences among Mountain View, Chicago, New York, San Bruno, San Francisco, and Sunnyvale.
- **Timeline:** interviews run on a rolling basis through Q1 2027. The internship is 12 consecutive weeks starting in May or June 2027.
- **Pay:** the US range is $58,000–$77,000, annualized.
- **Who it's for:** anyone enrolled full-time in a bachelor's program can apply, but it's aimed at students in their penultimate year.
- **What to submit:** a resume with your expected graduation date in MM/YY format, and a transcript (unofficial is fine). To upload the transcript, choose "Now attending" under Degree Status. PDFs are preferred.

Since applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, it's better to apply well before October 9.

> *Claude used WebSearch: Google sales intern Summer 2027 "Google Customer Solutions" OR "Sales Associate" intern*

Yes, and the Business Undergraduate Intern posting is still the right one for a sales internship. Google doesn't publish a separate US sales intern listing. Sales is one of the three tracks in that program, alongside Marketing and Finance, and its description covers the GCS-type work: account managers, consultants, and analysts helping small businesses and placing product ads where they'll be most useful. You apply once, and after the application window closes Google may contact you to ask which function you prefer. That's when you'd say Sales, and specifically GCS.

A US Summer 2027 intern posting only for GCS doesn't seem to exist. The only GCS-specific intern listing I found is the Customer Growth Associate internship, which is for students in the EMEA region.

**If you're graduating by 2027**, GCS has a full-time role that follows directly from the internship: **[Customer Growth Associate, Google Customer Solutions, University Graduate, 2027 Start](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/121628144896484038-customer-growth-associate/)**. You'd act as a sales advisor to small and mid-sized businesses and agency partners, manage a large number of accounts, and use phone and email outreach to hit quarterly revenue goals. You'd also go through training and mentorship with a cohort of other new hires.
