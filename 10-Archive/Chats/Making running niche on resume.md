### Claude · 2026-09-24 17:37

The trick is to swap the generic word for the specific version of it that *you* actually do. "Running" tells a reader nothing; a detail gives them something to remember and ask about in an interview. A few angles to try:

**Name the distance or format**
- Half-marathon runner (PR 1:42)
- Trail and ultramarathon running
- 5K track racing
- Mile repeats and interval training

**Add a place or setting**
- Trail running in Florida's state parks (e.g., San Felasco)
- Early-morning campus loop runs
- Running every bridge in a city you've visited

**Add a goal, streak, or achievement**
- Completed 3 marathons, training for a Boston qualifier
- 400-day running streak
- Ran a race in 6 different states

**Add a social or community angle**
- Pace group leader for a local run club
- Volunteer race-course marshal
- Organizing Saturday long runs with coworkers

**Combine it with another interest**
- Running food tours (the best post-run breakfast spots)
- Running and photography: documenting sunrise routes
- Tracking training data in spreadsheets / Strava analytics

A strong interest line usually has one concrete detail, like a number, a place, or a role. "Trail running (two 50K finishes)" beats "Running, hiking, fitness."

If you tell me what kind of running you actually do and what field you're applying to, I can help you phrase it so it also hints at traits employers like (discipline, consistency, leadership).

### Claude · 2026-09-24 17:51

Every role on your resume is marked "Present," so the standard is **present tense for ongoing work**. Past tense is only correct for things that are finished, like a promotion or a role you've moved on from. Right now your Work Experience section is almost all past tense, while your Leadership section is mostly present, so that's the main inconsistency.

## Tense fixes (revised bullets)

**Global Source Data Solutions**
- Build and operate an automated email campaign pipeline (n8n, Instantly, Slack, Anthropic API) that cuts launch time from a full day to under 10 minutes, enabling 2-3 campaigns daily with 4-email sequences screened against a 150-entry suppression list.
- Develop internal automation tools and dashboards (Claude, Gemini, Lovable) across a 14-product catalog of 301M+ consumer records and 486M+ B2B contacts in 150+ countries, including an expense tracker and client data intake form.
- Launch email campaigns to 3,000+ prospects, grow brand presence across LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and the Monda data marketplace, and revitalize 40+ website pages spanning 8 industry verticals following a full technical SEO audit.
- Conduct beta QA testing on Octopus DaaS / Audience Builder as one of its first users, logging bugs and UX feedback through GitHub to speed launch, and maintain CRM data hygiene in ServiceFlow across the company's active pipeline.

**Volvera AI**
- Lead an AI consultancy brand built on a 3-stage process that lowers client costs, offering 19+ automation services across 5 product lines through a freemium model.
- Build cold email programs end to end for 4 clients, from prospect lists modeled on customers they've already closed, to separate sequences for each group, to reply tracking that shows which version is booking meetings.
- Built a review system that texts customers a 1-tap review link when a job is marked complete, and run a weekly newsletter that keeps clients in front of every lead who received a quote but never booked.
- Rebuild client CRM structures and workflows so the pipeline shows each representative's full impact, and build the landing pages their ads point to, raising conversion rates for web traffic.

**Florida Tech Pathways**
- Collaborate with a team of 4 analysts to deliver insights for recruitment initiatives, using SQL and Excel to clean, organize, and validate data, contributing to a 24% increase in applications.

These can **stay past tense** because they describe finished events:
- "Promoted to Assistant Comptroller…" (ACCENT)
- "Led marketing, participant coordination… as a Staffer before March 2026 promotion" (Homecoming)
- "Selected as a Tech Sales Analyst…" (Florida Tech Pathways)

## Other discrepancies I noticed

1. **Homecoming dates:** The title says Assistant Director since September 2025, but the second bullet says you were promoted in March 2026. Consider "Assistant Director of Web Design, March 2026 – Present" and "Staffer, September 2025 – March 2026."
2. **"Every year" (Homecoming):** You've been there for one cycle, so "every year" overstates it. "On deadline" alone works.
3. **Florida Tech Pathways timing:** The program started this month and runs 10 weeks, so a 24% increase in applications this early may raise questions in an interview. Make sure you can explain where that number comes from.
4. **Volvera "Founded" vs. "Co-Founder":** Your original bullet said "Founded," but your title says Co-Founder. My revision avoids the conflict.
5. **"Ensuring positive customer satisfaction":** This is unsupported and a bit redundant, so I dropped it. Add a metric if you have one, such as the number of reviews gained.
6. **Minor line:** An AI Certificate isn't a minor. Try "Minor: Professional Selling | Certificate: Artificial Intelligence."
7. **Heavener vs. Warrington:** Your education line says Heavener School of Business, but your Senate bullet says Warrington College of Business. Both are technically correct, since Heavener sits within Warrington, but a recruiter may wonder. Keep Warrington in the Senate bullet (that's the seat) or add "Warrington College of Business" to your education line.
8. **Number style:** You write both "four-email" and "one term" in words and "4 clients" and "4 analysts" as numerals. Pick numerals throughout, since that's the resume norm.
9. **Oxford comma:** Some lists use it ("Instagram, and the Monda…") and some don't ("Review, debate and vote"; "HTML, CSS and UX"). Pick one style.
10. **Capitalization in Interests:** "Vinyl collecting" is lowercase while the other interests are Title Case, so change it to "Vinyl Collecting."
11. **Formatting:** There are double spaces in "Minor:  Professional" and "Lovable,  n8n." The Florida Tech Pathways title line also has a long run of tabs, so check that its date aligns with the others once it's in your template.
