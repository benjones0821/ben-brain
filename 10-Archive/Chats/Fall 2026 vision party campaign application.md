### Claude · 2026-08-07 14:58

Good instinct — a tracker and a supply closet are things any competent committee already runs. Here's what I'd propose instead.

**For Director of Accountability**, four options:

- **Peer pods** — group candidates into squads of four or five who table together and cover for each other. People show up for their friends more reliably than for a committee.
- **Points leaderboard** — shifts, shares, and org meetings earn points, posted weekly. Turns compliance into competition rather than nagging.
- **Shift-swap board** — most no-shows are class conflicts, not defiance. Give candidates a place to trade shifts so they swap instead of ghosting.
- **Week-two check-ins** — a ten-minute one-on-one with every candidate early on, to catch who's drifting while it's still fixable.

**For Logistics**, four options:

- **Zone captains** — split campus into zones with a materials captain in each, so distribution isn't one person driving to everything.
- **Storage drop points** — negotiate two or three storage spots near high-traffic tabling areas so materials don't make a round trip daily.
- **Rain plan** — it's Gainesville in the fall. Pre-picked indoor backup locations and waterproofed material storage.
- **Election-week surge plan** — the final week needs several times the normal throughput, and that gets planned in week one, not week five.

Pick one or two per committee and I'll write them up with implementation steps:
