### Claude · 2026-09-20 17:05

Relish is a build-your-own burger joint — you pick from around forty fresh ingredients and they build it. No published nutrition info, so you'd be guessing on macros.

You can still make it work there:
- Double beef patty (~2/3 lb = ~50g protein), lettuce wrap or ditch the top bun
- Load the free veggies — onion, pickles, jalapeño, lettuce, tomato
- Mustard or hot sauce only; skip Big Tasty sauce, garlic mayo, cheese
- No fries, no onion rings, no shake

That lands you somewhere around 500–600 cal with 45–55g protein, but it's an estimate.

**Verdict:** if you actually care about hitting numbers today, Chipotle. If you want a burger and are willing to eat approximately well, Relish is fine — just don't order the Big Tasty (double patty, double cheese, double sauce, and no substitutions allowed).

≈250 cal, ~34g protein

Numbers are estimates from standard patty composition, not from Relish. If you want certainty on macros, Chipotle is still the only one that publishes real data.
