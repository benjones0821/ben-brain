## What makes a good topic for this assignment

Exercises 3 and 4 work best with a topic that has **specific, checkable claims** where AI answers can be vague, outdated, or wrong on the first try. You want questions that improve noticeably with better prompting (Exercise 3) and have real sources to verify against (Exercise 4). Avoid anything purely opinion-based or so settled that there's nothing to investigate.

## Topic ideas with one question per member

**1. Sleep and health**
- Does blue light from screens actually disrupt sleep?
- Do you really need 8 hours, or does it vary by person?
- Can you "catch up" on lost sleep over the weekend?
- Does melatonin work for general insomnia?

**2. Microplastics**
- How much plastic does the average person ingest per week? (The "credit card a week" claim is great to test.)
- Are microplastics found in human brains or blood?
- Do bottled water drinkers ingest more microplastics?
- Is there evidence microplastics harm human health?

**3. Nutrition claims**
- Is intermittent fasting better for weight loss than regular calorie restriction?
- Are seed oils harmful?
- Does sugar cause hyperactivity in kids?
- Is red meat linked to cancer?

**4. Climate and energy**
- Are electric cars actually better for the environment over their lifetime?
- Is nuclear power safer than other energy sources per unit of energy?
- Do paper bags have a lower footprint than plastic ones?
- Is recycling plastic effective?

**5. Screens and young people**
- Does social media cause depression in teenagers?
- Does screen time harm child development?
- Do video games increase aggression?
- Do phone bans in schools improve grades?

Microplastics and screens/teens are especially strong picks: the evidence is active and contested, and viral claims are easy to test.

- Can restoring wetlands and mangroves store more carbon than forests?

**Big-picture and controversial**
- Is solar geoengineering (reflecting sunlight) a viable backup plan?
- Is it still possible to limit warming to 1.5°C?
- Do individual actions matter, or is change only possible through governments and corporations?

**Strong picks for this assignment:** the seaweed-fed cows, wind turbines and birds, heat pumps in cold climates, and 1.5°C questions. Each has a specific claim that's easy to test with AI and then verify with SIFT.
