You're not a student cold-messaging a founder; you're an insider at his ICP who can tell him things he pays to learn. Lead with that.

A few other real threads: he ran Fathom, which helped consumers *pull* their data back from companies like yours, then sold it to Delphia. He's CIPP/US certified. He runs an arts nonprofit explicitly framed around mental health — you're a Thrive IFC mental health ambassador. He's mentored kids at Hacker Fund for ten years, so he's mentor-inclined. And you have mutual connections (Daniel, Nash), so a warm intro is available.

**The questions that make you interesting to him**

- State data broker registration laws are multiplying — from where you sit, are companies actually registering, or is it still mostly unenforced? *(This is a question he wants your answer to. Asking it is a gift.)*
- What happens to MAID-based businesses over the next five years? Is that a category with a floor, or is it slowly being legislated out?
- Superset's pitch is AI agents built on real expert workflows. What breaks when you try to encode a privacy attorney's judgment into an agent — where does it still need a human?
- You went from building Fathom, which helped people reclaim data *from* brokers, to selling compliance *to* brokers. Was that a change in view or the same thesis from the other side?

**Questions on his public positions** (shows you read, not just skimmed)

- He predicted Granola gets sued by 2027 over no-consent recording. Ask if he still holds that, and what he thinks the first enforcement action in that category looks like.
- He wrote that SOC2 has lost its luster and guessed ISO or AI-pentesting comes next. Ask what he'd actually want to see replace it — and whether Superset would build toward that.
- He posted about the geofencing/Fourth Amendment decision. Ask how much government-access rulings actually change what commercial data brokers can do.

**Career questions, once you've earned the floor**

- He's a coder who became a seller because "app stores are full of great tech nobody downloads." For someone in an IS program: how much of the technical depth was load-bearing, and how much was the go-to-market skill?
- Is CIPP/US worth it early, or is it credentialing that only matters once you have something to attach it to?
- He was doing DDoS research and cybersecurity engineering at your age, roughly. What would he tell a rising sophomore to spend the next two summers on?

**One human one**

- Palooza is arts programming aimed at mental health. You do mental health outreach in Greek life. Ask what he's learned about what actually moves people versus what just looks like it does.

**How to play it**

Two cautions. First, don't offer him an intro to GSDSI or discuss anything internal — you're an apprentice, that's not yours to trade, and offering it makes the conversation transactional in a way that will backfire. Talk about the *industry*, not your employer's specifics. Second, don't ask for anything in the first message. Send three sentences: who you are, the fact that you work inside the data brokerage stack he sells into, and one specific question. He posts constantly and gets decent engagement — he'll answer a good question from someone who clearly did the reading.
