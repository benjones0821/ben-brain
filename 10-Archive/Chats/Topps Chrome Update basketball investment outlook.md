### Claude · 2026-08-03 16:24

**Rip — or don't buy boxes at all.**

Holding is the weakest of the three options here. The table shows post-2019 sealed retail returning 1–2.5x over five to seven years, illiquid, sometimes negative. That's a bad trade for money you're locking up, and Chrome Update is on the wrong side of every variable that made 2018-19 work.

The honest ranking for this product:

1. **Buy singles after release.** If what you actually want is Flagg cards, they get cheaper once breakers flood the market. Best dollar-for-dollar outcome.
2. **Rip a Value or Mega at retail.** You're paying $45–85 for the experience and a lottery ticket. Expected value is below purchase price — that's true of every sealed product — but you get the thing you paid for.
3. **Hold sealed.** Only defensible if you get shrink-wrapped boxes at retail price and genuinely don't care about the money for 5+ years.

If you rip, the pull that matters is a Flagg Debut Patch 1/1 — and the odds on that are effectively zero per box. Everything else you pull, you could have bought cheaper in three months.

So: buy one or two at retail, rip them because ripping is the fun part, and put anything you actually want on a want list for October.
